Anticline Collective is a temporary artist collective in the Gorge -- a real storefront, movable walls, and a crew of local artists showing work made here, about here, and about everything else. The storefront is borrowed time, and we're fine with that, we'll do pop-ups as well.

Get Involved Why "Anticline"?

The Name

Everyone in the Gorge calls Coyote Wall "the Syncline." One problem: it's an anticline.

The great wall of basalt above Bingen is folded rock pushed up -- geologists call it the Bingen Anticline. The actual syncline, the downfold, sits under the Columbia River where nobody rides bikes. The nickname stuck anyway.

We're named for the thing everybody sees and almost nobody calls by its right name -- a little contrary on purpose. Temporary fits the same streak: borrowed storefront, no forever lease, no museum pretensions. Just work on the wall while the window's open.

How It Works

A gallery with wheels under everything and no illusions about permanence.

01

Commission, not rent

No monthly payment hanging over anybody. When work sells, the space and the collective take a cut. Sell nothing, owe nothing. Final split is being worked out -- join the conversation and help shape it.

02

Movable by design

Walls, panels, and display fixtures that break down and load into a truck. The same kit that fills the storefront works a First Friday in the Gorge or a Last Thursday in White Salmon or Alberta.

03

Shared stewardship

Artists help run the thing: hanging, open hours, popups -- not just dropping work and vanishing. The salon and esthetician who share the building keep daily eyes on the floor and earn commission for it. Who does what, and how it's valued, is still open. Show up and help write the rules.

04

Honest terms

About 800 square feet, ours until a long-term tenant signs. Security cameras are planned, and we're sorting coverage for work on display. Everything is built to survive a move -- including the name.
